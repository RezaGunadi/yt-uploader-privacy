Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how this application (the “App”, “we”) handles information when it is used to upload, manage, and schedule videos on our own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API v3.

1. Scope

The App is a personal tool operated by the channel owner. It is used solely to upload, update, and schedule videos on YouTube channels that we own and control. The App has no end users other than the channel owner.

2. Information we access

When authorized through Google OAuth 2.0 (scope youtube.force-ssl), the App accesses only the Google/YouTube account of the authenticated channel owner, in order to:

  • upload videos produced by the owner;
  • read and update video metadata (title, description, tags, thumbnail);
  • set privacy status and schedule publication (publishAt);
  • read the owner’s own channel and video information needed for the above.

The App does not access, request, or process data belonging to any other user.

3. How we use information

Accessed information is used exclusively to perform the upload, update, and scheduling actions described above, on the owner’s own channel. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to managing the owner’s own videos.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any accessed information to third parties. No data is shared with anyone.

5. Data storage and security

OAuth credentials (access and refresh tokens) are stored locally on the owner’s own computer and are used only to authenticate API requests. They are not transmitted to any third party.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The App’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Data retention and deletion

The only stored data is the owner’s own OAuth token, kept locally for as long as the App is in use. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the token can be deleted by removing the local token file.
